Capacity and interpretation:
The current CHRs have free licenses. Their documented upload limit is 1 Mbps
per interface, so this test cannot create real congestion at configured 8/4 Gbps.

profile or higher licensed real throughput. The saved WAN capacities will not
be silently reduced, and generated traffic is not proof of automatic routing.
A before/after routing improvement will not be claimed while execution is off.
