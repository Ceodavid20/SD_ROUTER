How the figures are used

Averages use the current uninterrupted counter series. The previous average ends one sample earlier. A change from a zero previous average has no percentage value.

Path shares are calculated within each aggregation router's confirmed paths. Costs use confirmed carrier prices per decimal GB and project the average rate over one hour.
